— Archive Overview

The Carpets of the Museum of Islamic Art presents a curated selection of historical carpets from the collection of the Museum of Islamic Art in Doha, Qatar. Each piece reflects the rich textile heritage of the Islamic world, spanning regions from Persia to Anatolia and centuries of craftsmanship. The archive aims to make these masterpieces accessible to researchers, students, and enthusiasts worldwide, celebrating the artistry woven into every thread..
